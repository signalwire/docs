## Brand Identity Verification

When a Brand is submitted, The Campaign Registry automatically validates the **EIN**, **Brand Legal
Name**, and **EIN Issuing Country** against independent third-party identity data sources. The
result is the Brand's Identity Status, available as soon as the submission finishes processing.

Identity verification decides whether a Brand can have Campaigns at all:

| TCR Identity Status | SignalWire brand state | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| — | `pending` | The submission is still processing. This is an interim label, not a verification result. |
| `VERIFIED` | `completed` | Brand identity was confirmed. Campaigns can now be registered. |
| `UNVERIFIED` | `unverified` | The Brand could not be matched against third-party records. |

<Warning>
Campaigns cannot be created for a Brand that is `pending` or `unverified`. Identity verification is
the first gate — every later step, including DCA election, is blocked until the Brand reaches
`completed`.
</Warning>

If you set a `status_callback_url` on the Brand, SignalWire sends a `brand_activated` event when the
Brand reaches `completed` and a `brand_unverified` event when verification fails. See the
[10DLC status callback](/docs/apis/rest/campaign-registry/webhooks/ten-dlc-status-callback)
reference.

### Why a Brand comes back unverified

The most common cause is a mismatch between the Brand Legal Name and the EIN as filed with the
federal government — a DBA or marketing name entered in the legal name field, a typo in the EIN, or
the wrong EIN Issuing Country.

A Brand can also come back unverified when every field is correct:

- **A recently issued EIN.** New EINs take time to propagate into the third-party datasets TCR
  checks. Until they do, a correct EIN will not match.
- **Non-US entities.** TCR's initial verification is frequently insufficient for entities outside
  the US, and international Brands return unverified at a high rate.
- **Non-profits.** A 501(c) organization may verify while its Tax Exempt Status — 501(c)(3), (4),
  (5), or (6) — fails to resolve.

### Resolving an unverified Brand

There are three paths. Correct any inaccurate Brand details *before* resubmitting or appealing.

1. **Correct the details and resubmit.** Use this when something was genuinely wrong. Every
   resubmission is billed as a new Brand registration — $4 per submission when registering through
   SignalWire. Resubmitting unchanged or still-inaccurate details will fail again and be charged
   again, so appeal instead if the details are already correct.
2. **File an identity status appeal with supporting documentation.** Use this when the details are
   already correct — for example a recently issued EIN that has not yet propagated. Attach the IRS
   CP 575 EIN confirmation letter or equivalent formation documentation. An appeal is also the
   expected path for international Brands and for a 501(c) Tax Exempt Status that will not resolve.
3. **Request or import external vetting.** A standard vet by an approved vetting provider
   establishes identity independently, and may also unlock additional MNO classes and throughput
   tiers. Third-party vetting fees apply.

None of these are instant. Allow several business days for an appeal or a vet to come back.
