<Note title="Identity verification and DCA election are separate gates">

**Brand Identity Verification** validates *who the Brand is*. It runs at The Campaign Registry,
against third-party identity data, and must pass before a Campaign can exist at all.

**DCA election** validates *what the Campaign sends*. It runs after the Campaign is loaded into
your SignalWire Space, and reviews the website, use case, message content, and CTIA compliance.

The two are run by different parties and have different remedies. Entity documentation such as an
IRS CP 575 belongs in a TCR identity status appeal — the DCA never reviews it. If your Brand is
unverified, DCA election is not the gate you are waiting on.

</Note>
