Adding a payment method to your Space turns on Auto Top-Up.
When your balance falls below a threshold you set, SignalWire charges the card you chose and adds the funds to your balance.
This keeps calls and messages running, and keeps recurring charges such as phone numbers paid.

<Warning title="Auto Top-Up is required">
Auto Top-Up is required for any account with active SignalWire resources.
You can change the amount, the threshold, and the card that funds it.
</Warning>

## When you are charged

| Setting | Default | Limits |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Threshold | `$5` | Minimum `$5`, maximum `$1,000,000` |
| Top-up amount | `$10` | Minimum `$10` |

The charge is normally the top-up amount.
If topping up by that amount would still leave your balance at or below the threshold, SignalWire charges enough to clear the threshold and add the top-up amount on top.
The amount charged can therefore be larger than the amount you configured.

With a threshold of `$50` and a top-up amount of `$10`, a balance that falls to `$5` is charged `$55`.

Auto Top-Up charges at most once every six hours.
A Space that spends faster than that can reach a zero balance between charges and lose service until the next one, so set a top-up amount that covers six hours of your own traffic.

Some features require Auto Top-Up to be active before you can use them.
Registering a campaign with [The Campaign Registry](/docs/platform/messaging/campaign-registry) is one of them, and returns the `auto_top_up_deactivated` error while Auto Top-Up is off.

## Change your settings

Open **Billing → Low Balance Settings** in the Dashboard to change the top-up amount, the threshold, and which card funds Auto Top-Up.
The same screen sets up [low balance notifications](#low-balance-notifications), an email or webhook sent on the same low-balance event.

## Low balance notifications

A balance that runs out stops your calls and messages.
SignalWire can warn you before that happens by sending an email, a webhook, or both when your balance falls below your threshold.

### When notifications are sent

The email and the webhook fire on the same low-balance event as Auto Top-Up, so the same cadence applies: at most once every six hours.
They're sent whether or not Auto Top-Up is on.

The email goes to every administrator of your Space.

### Turn notifications on

In **Billing → Low Balance Settings**, turn on the email, the webhook, or both, and set the webhook's URL and HTTP method.

To manage these settings over the REST API, use [Retrieve low balance settings][get-setting] and [Update low balance settings][update-setting].
The `send_email`, `send_webhook`, `webhook_url`, and `webhook_method` fields control notifications.
An update replaces the whole setting, so send your threshold and Auto Top-Up fields along with the notification fields.

### The webhook request

SignalWire sends an HTTP request to your webhook URL with two form-encoded parameters.
With `GET`, they arrive as query parameters.
With `POST`, they arrive in the request body.

| Parameter | Description | Example |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `threshold` | Your low balance threshold, in US dollars. | `5.0` |
| `balance` | The balance that triggered the notification, in US dollars. | `4.253117` |

The request carries only these two parameters, with the `User-Agent` header `SignalwireCallback/1.0`.

SignalWire allows 3 seconds to connect to your endpoint and 5 seconds for the whole request, and follows up to 3 redirects.

SignalWire doesn't retry a failed delivery.
If your endpoint is down, times out, or returns an error, that notification is lost, and the next one comes with the next low-balance event.
To check where your balance stands after an outage, use [Retrieve the balance][get-balance].

## Remove the card that funds Auto Top-Up

A card cannot be removed while it is your Auto Top-Up payment method. To remove it:

<Steps>

### Add another card

Add the replacement payment method to your Space.

### Point Auto Top-Up at the new card

In Low Balance Settings, select the new card as your Auto Top-Up payment method and save.

### Remove the original card

The original card can now be removed from the Payment Methods page.

</Steps>

## If a charge fails

If the charge is declined, or no valid card is on file to charge, SignalWire turns Auto Top-Up off and emails your Space administrators.
Adding a payment method or topping up your balance manually turns it back on.
