Send and receive faxes on the same phone numbers, SWML documents, and Relay clients you use for
voice calls. A fax is a call: SignalWire answers or places it, transfers the pages, stores what you
receive as a PDF, and reports the result to your document or your code.

<Tip title="Security first" icon="lock">SignalWire Fax is certified [**SOC 2 Type II**](https://signalwire.com/blogs/industry/soc-2-type-ii-attestation) compliant, and is built from the ground up for seamless **HIPAA** compliance.</Tip>
