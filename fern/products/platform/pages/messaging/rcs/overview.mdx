Rich Communication Services (RCS) messages arrive in the recipient's default messaging app from a
branded sender: your brand name, logo, color, and description appear alongside the conversation. On
top of plain text, an RCS message can carry rich content such as cards, carousels, media, locations,
and suggestions the recipient taps to reply, open a link, call you, or add an event to their
calendar.

RCS messages go through the same [Messaging API](/docs/apis/rest/messages/create-message) endpoint
you use for SMS, MMS, and WhatsApp, so RCS becomes one more channel alongside the ones you already
run.

## How it works

The sender of an RCS message is an **RCS agent**, not a phone number. You register an agent from the
Dashboard with your brand details, and SignalWire takes it through approval with you:

1. **Set up.** You submit the agent's registration form. The agent's status is `pending`.
2. **Test.** SignalWire provisions the agent for testing and its status becomes `in_review`. You can
   send messages through the API to the test device numbers registered on the agent, using the
   agent's ID as `from`.
3. **Launch.** SignalWire applies to the mobile operators in the United States and the status
   becomes `launching`. Once the operators approve it, the agent is `launched` and can message any
   US subscriber on those networks.

<Note>
RCS messaging is available only from your agent to US phone numbers for now. Sends to any other
country, including Canada and US territories such as Puerto Rico, are rejected.
</Note>

Inbound RCS messages reach your application the same way inbound SMS does: assign the agent to a
[Resource](/docs/platform/resources) and the Resource handles messages sent to the agent, for
example by replying from a SWML script.

## Prerequisites

To register an RCS agent, have these ready:

- Your organization's name and legal entity name, and a contact for the registration.
- Your brand name, description, and color, plus image URLs for a 224 × 224 px logo and a
  1440 × 448 px banner.
- URLs for your privacy policy and terms and conditions.
- The phone numbers of the devices you want to test with before launch.
