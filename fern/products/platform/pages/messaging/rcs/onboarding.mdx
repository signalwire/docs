An RCS agent is the branded sender your recipients see. Registering one starts from the Dashboard;
SignalWire then provisions the agent for testing and handles the launch with mobile operators.

## Prerequisites

Have the details listed under [Prerequisites](/docs/platform/messaging/rcs#prerequisites) ready,
including image URLs for your logo and banner and the phone numbers of your test devices.

## Fees

Setting up and testing an agent carries no registration fees. Messages sent during testing are
billed at standard RCS messaging rates.

Fees apply once the agent is ready to [launch](#launch-the-agent) for production use. Launching and
maintaining an agent carries an annual \$100 brand vetting and certification fee. If the agent will
message T-Mobile lines, a \$500 T-Mobile Campaign Service Activation/Migration fee is also due at
initial launch. Resubmitting the registration or updating brand assets incurs additional fees. Fees
are subject to change.

## Submit the registration

<Steps>

### Open the registration form

In your [SignalWire Dashboard](https://my.signalwire.com), go to **Messaging Campaigns → RCS Agents**
and select **Add New**.

The **RCS Agents** menu item appears only once RCS agent registration is enabled for your Space. If
you don't see it, contact [Support](https://support.signalwire.com/portal) to have it enabled.

### Fill in the form

The form has these sections, in this order:

| Section | What it asks for |
|---|---|
| **Organization** | Your organization's name and legal entity name, and whether you're registering on behalf of another brand. |
| **Contact** | The name, title, email address, and phone number of the person to contact about the registration. |
| **Branding Information** | The brand name, description, color, logo, and banner shown on the recipient's device, and your privacy policy and terms and conditions URLs. |
| **Contact Methods** | At least one phone number, email address, or website shown on the agent so recipients can reach you, each with an optional label. |
| **Messaging Setup** | The phone numbers of your RCS test devices, and optionally an SMS Sender ID and a callback or webhook URL. |
| **Sender Agent Use** | The billing category and the agent's use case. |
| **Launch Details** | How you obtain user opt-in, what triggers your messages, the interactions you expect, and your opt-out confirmation message. Mobile operators review these answers before approving the agent for launch. |
| **Agent Preview** | How a reviewer can try the experience: a tester opt-in URL, an opt-out video URL, or a screenshots URL. |
| **Launch Countries** | Where the agent launches, and other countries where you'd like RCS. |

A preview beside the form shows how your agent appears on a device as you fill it in.

Under **Launch Countries**, the United States is always selected: RCS messaging is available only to
US phone numbers for now. The countries you pick under **Other Geographic Areas of Interest** are
saved with your registration to tell us where you'd like RCS next. The agent doesn't launch there.

### Submit

Confirm that you're authorized to register the agent on behalf of your organization, then select
**Register**. The agent's page opens with the status `pending`, and the agent is listed under
**RCS Agents**.

</Steps>

## Test the agent

When SignalWire has provisioned your agent for testing, its status changes to `in_review` and the
agent's page shows its **Agent ID**. Until the agent is launched, it can send messages only to the
test device numbers registered on it.

[Send a message](/docs/platform/messaging/rcs/send-messages) with the Agent ID as `from` and one of
your test numbers as `to`, and confirm it arrives on that device with your branding. If the API
returns `rcs_agent_not_sendable`, the agent isn't in `in_review`, `launching`, or `launched`. If it returns
`rcs_agent_not_configured`, the agent hasn't finished provisioning for testing.

## Launch the agent

After testing, SignalWire applies to the mobile operators in the United States and
the agent's status becomes `launching`. Once the operators approve it, the status becomes `launched`
and the agent can message any US subscriber on those networks.

## Agent statuses

| Status | Meaning | Can send |
|---|---|---|
| `pending` | The registration was submitted and is waiting to be provisioned. | No |
| `in_review` | The agent is provisioned for testing. | To test devices only |
| `launching` | SignalWire is applying to mobile operators in the United States. | To test devices only |
| `launched` | Mobile operators approved the agent. | To any US subscriber on those networks |
| `rejected` | The registration was not approved. | No |
| `suspended` | The agent was suspended. | No |
