## Click-to-Call widget builder added to the Dashboard

Create and manage [Click-to-Call widgets](/docs/browser-sdk/guides/click-to-call-widget)
from the Dashboard under **Tools** > **Click To Call**. A widget's button can start an audio
call, open a video room, or connect to an AI agent, and the widget works on all devices.

## More voices available for AI agents

Choose from a larger selection of voices when you configure an AI agent, in a simpler voice
picker.
