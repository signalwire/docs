## Help and Support panel added to the Dashboard

Open the Help and Support panel from the expand caret on the right side of any page in your Space.
It gives you the community Discord, the developer documentation, and the support portal, where you can submit a ticket and view your existing tickets.

## IDs in Dashboard logs labeled by type

Log details now label each ID by its type, such as Call ID or Message ID, so you can tell at a glance which kind of ID you have.
