## Phone number release allowance changed to two early releases per month

Non-trial accounts can release up to two phone numbers each month within 14 days of purchase.
Any additional number you release that month is subject to the standard 14-day hold after
purchase, with no exceptions, to help prevent fraud and abuse.

Release a number from its page in the [Phone Numbers](/docs/platform/phone-numbers) section of
the Dashboard.
