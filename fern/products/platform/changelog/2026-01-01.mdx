## Toll-free verification requires business registration details

New [toll-free verification](/docs/platform/messaging/toll-free-numbers) submissions must include three fields that were optional before January 1, 2026:

- **Business Registration Number**: your company's EIN or other government-issued identifier.
- **Issuing Country of Business Registration**: the country where your business is legally registered.
- **Entity Type**: Public Profit, Private Profit, Non-Profit, Government, or Sole Proprietor.

Numbers that are already verified aren't affected.

## Updated Cloud Agreement must be accepted to use the Dashboard

An updated SignalWire Cloud Agreement takes effect January 1, 2026.
The Dashboard prompts you to review it, and once any user in your Space selects **I Agree**, everyone in the Space has full access again.
Continuing to access or use SignalWire's services also constitutes agreement to the updated terms.
