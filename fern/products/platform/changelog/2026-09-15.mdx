## AI Sidecar added to SWML

Attach an AI observer to a live call with the
[`ai_sidecar`](/docs/swml/reference/calling/ai-sidecar) SWML method. The sidecar listens to
both sides, evaluates the conversation after each customer turn, and sends agent-facing output
to your application as webhook callbacks. It never speaks on the call.

- Define what to watch for with `prompt`, and set which leg is the customer with `customer_role`.
- Output arrives as [webhook events](/docs/apis/rest/webhooks/ai-sidecar-callback), including `insight`, `turn`, `tool_call`, and `final`.
- Tool calling through SWAIG functions and MCP servers lets the sidecar act on your systems mid-call.

Use it for live agent coaching, compliance flags, supervisor alerts, or CRM triggers.
`ai_sidecar` and `live_transcribe` can't run on the same call.
