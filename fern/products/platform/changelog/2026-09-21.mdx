## Opus bitrate options clarified in Dashboard SIP codec settings

The codec picker for [SIP endpoints](/docs/platform/voice/sip/sip-credentials) and
[SIP gateways](/docs/platform/voice/sip) in the Dashboard now labels each Opus option with the
bitrate it uses, so you can tell the variants apart before you save. Previously the options
looked alike and gave no indication of which bitrate a given choice applied to the call.
Existing endpoint and gateway configurations are unchanged, and codecs you already selected keep
working.
