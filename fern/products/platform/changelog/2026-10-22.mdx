## Search added to the Set an Emergency Address screen

Search your E911 addresses by label and address on the **Set an Emergency Address** screen in the
Dashboard, instead of scrolling through every record to find the one to assign.

To manage emergency addresses and assign them to phone numbers, read
[Set up E911 in the Dashboard](/docs/platform/e911#set-up-e911-in-the-dashboard).
