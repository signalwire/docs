## Trial credit now expires 90 days after Space creation

Trial credit expires 90 days after your Space is created if the Space is still in
[trial mode](/docs/platform/trial-mode). Any balance left at that point is removed.

## LaML renamed to cXML

LaML is now called cXML across the SignalWire documentation, APIs, and Dashboard.
Only the name changes. See the [cXML reference](/docs/compatibility-api/cxml).

## Direct URLs to recordings no longer exposed

Accessing media and recording files through the API or the Dashboard no longer exposes the direct URLs of
recordings.
