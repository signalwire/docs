## Subscriber resource added

Create and manage user accounts for your apps and services with the new Subscriber
resource. See [Subscribers](/docs/platform/subscribers).

## AI agents, call flows, and SIP endpoints managed from the Resources tab

Create, filter, and sort resources such as AI agents, call flows, and SIP endpoints from the
**Resources** tab in the Dashboard. See [Resources](/docs/platform/resources).

## `<Pay>` verb added for PCI-compliant card payments

Collect credit card payments on a call with the [`<Pay>`](/docs/compatibility-api/cxml/reference/voice/pay)
verb, which processes card details in a PCI-compliant way.
