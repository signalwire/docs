## RCS messaging added for US recipients

Send and receive [RCS messages](/docs/platform/messaging/rcs) to US phone numbers from a branded
RCS agent that shows your brand name, logo, and color in the recipient's messaging app. RCS
messages can carry cards, carousels, media, and suggested replies, and go through the same
[Messaging API](/docs/apis/rest/messages/create-message) endpoint as SMS and MMS. To get started,
[register an RCS agent](/docs/platform/messaging/rcs/onboarding) from the Dashboard.
