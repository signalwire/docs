## Six new text-to-speech engines

Groq, Mistral, Grok, Smallest.ai, Fish Audio, and Speechify are documented as text-to-speech engines, each with its models, voices, and usage. The `groq-tts` engine replaces the retired `playai-turbo`. Rime's `arcana` model is deprecated in favor of `coda`.
