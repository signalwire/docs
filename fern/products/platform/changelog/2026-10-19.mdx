## Automatic move of Platform Free Trial numbers to your own 10DLC Campaign added

The US 10DLC number you used for [Platform Free Trial](/docs/platform/messaging/platform-free-trial#moving-your-trial-number-to-your-own-campaign)
now moves to your own approved 10DLC Campaign automatically. You no longer need to open a Support ticket to keep
using that number after you register with The Campaign Registry.
