## Self-service move of Platform Free Trial numbers to your own 10DLC Campaign added

Move the US 10DLC number you used for [Platform Free Trial](/docs/platform/messaging/platform-free-trial#moving-your-trial-number-to-your-own-campaign)
to your own approved 10DLC Campaign yourself: assign the number to the Campaign, and SignalWire moves it off Platform
Free Trial with no Support ticket needed. Once moved, the trial restrictions no longer apply, and the number's messaging
limits are those of the Campaign you assigned it to.
