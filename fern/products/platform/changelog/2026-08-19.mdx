## `ai.multilingual` added for per-language voices

Give an AI agent a voice per language and it detects what the caller speaks, answers in it, and switches whenever the caller does. `multilingual` and `languages` are mutually exclusive: set both and SignalWire uses `multilingual`, ignoring `languages` entirely.

See [`ai.multilingual`](/docs/swml/reference/calling/ai/multilingual).
