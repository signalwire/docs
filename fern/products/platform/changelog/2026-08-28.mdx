## Brand Identity Verification documented as its own gate

10DLC registration has two separate approval gates, and the docs described only one. A new section covers Brand Identity Verification: what The Campaign Registry checks against third-party identity data, the `pending`, `unverified`, and `completed` states, and the `brand_activated` and `brand_unverified` status callbacks. Campaigns cannot be created until a Brand reaches `completed`, which is upstream of DCA election entirely.

The section also explains why a Brand with every field correct can still come back unverified — a recently issued EIN, a non-US entity, or 501(c) tax-exempt status — and gives the three remedies: correct and resubmit, appeal with supporting documentation, or external vetting.
