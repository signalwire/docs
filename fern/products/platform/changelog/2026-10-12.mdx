## Fixed: Relay `calling.pay` ignored `play` prompts and used the default prompts

Relay `calling.pay` silently ignored a `play` prompt and used the default prompts instead.
This is now fixed.

Set custom prompts with the `prompts` parameter of `pay` in the
[Python](/docs/server-sdks/reference/python/relay/call/pay) or
[TypeScript](/docs/server-sdks/reference/typescript/relay/call/pay) Relay SDK.
