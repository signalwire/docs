## Opus codec variants added to SIP codec settings

Choose an Opus variant (OPUS 48HZ, OPUS 24HZ, OPUS 16HZ, or OPUS 8HZ) in a SIP resource's custom codec settings.
New Spaces have Opus selected by default. Existing Spaces keep their current selection, and you can add Opus or a variant under **Codecs and Ciphers** by choosing **Custom Codecs**.

## Add Funds banner shown when your balance reaches zero
