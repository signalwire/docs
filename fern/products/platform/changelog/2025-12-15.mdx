## New US local numbers no longer have messaging enabled by default

US local (10DLC) numbers purchased on or after December 15, 2025 need messaging activated before they can send or receive text messages.
Numbers you already own with messaging aren't affected.

To send messages, register a campaign with [The Campaign Registry](/docs/platform/messaging/campaign-registry); messaging turns on when the number is assigned to an approved campaign.
To only receive messages, contact the Carrier Operations team at [support@signalwire.com](mailto:support@signalwire.com) to have messaging enabled without registering a campaign.
