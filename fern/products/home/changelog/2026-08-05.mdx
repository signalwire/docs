## Six more text-to-speech engines

Choose [Groq](/docs/platform/voice/tts/groq-tts), [Mistral](/docs/platform/voice/tts/mistral), [Grok](/docs/platform/voice/tts/grok), [Smallest.ai](/docs/platform/voice/tts/smallest), [Fish Audio](/docs/platform/voice/tts/fish), or [Speechify](/docs/platform/voice/tts/speechify) as your TTS engine, each with its own models and voices.

Name the engine in your voice string — `groq-tts.autumn`, `speechify.<voice>` — so your choice survives a change to the platform default.

## `playai-turbo` is now `groq-tts`

Groq retired PlayAI in December 2025 and the model stopped answering, so calls on `playai-turbo` were already failing. The engine is renamed `groq-tts` and repointed at Canopy Labs' Orpheus, defaulting to `canopylabs/orpheus-v1-english`.

Selecting `canopylabs/orpheus-arabic-saudi` requires accepting Canopy Labs' model terms in the [Groq console](https://console.groq.com/) first — requests fail until you do. See [Groq](/docs/platform/voice/tts/groq-tts) for the voice list.

## Rime `arcana` is served by `coda`

Rime retired `arcana`. Requests naming it are served by `coda`, so existing configurations keep working untouched. If you already name `coda` directly, it now receives the same sampling parameters as other Rime models — `repetition_penalty`, `temperature`, `top_p`, and `max_tokens`.

See [Rime](/docs/platform/voice/tts/rime).
