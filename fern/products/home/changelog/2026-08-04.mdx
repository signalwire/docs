## Status callbacks are documented as advisory, not realtime triggers

Status callbacks are best-effort notifications: one can arrive late or never arrive at all, and a missed callback is invisible to your server. Do not gate time-critical or business-critical actions solely on receiving one. The webhooks page describes the alternatives, including Relay over WebSocket, REST confirmation and reconciliation, and in-call flow logic.
