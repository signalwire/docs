## Brand Identity Verification

Not a new requirement — 10DLC registration has always had two separate approval gates, not one. Before a Campaign can exist at all, The Campaign Registry checks your EIN, Brand legal name, and EIN issuing country against third-party identity data. That gate sits upstream of DCA election. A Brand reads `pending`, `unverified`, or `completed`, and the `brand_activated` and `brand_unverified` callbacks tell you when it moves.

A Brand with every field correct can still come back unverified — a recently issued EIN, a non-US entity, or 501(c) tax-exempt status. Three remedies: correct and resubmit, appeal with supporting documentation, or external vetting. Read [Campaign Service Providers](/docs/platform/messaging/campaign-registry/campaign-service-providers) if you file with TCR directly, or [Registration](/docs/platform/messaging/campaign-registry/registration) if SignalWire manages your Brand.
