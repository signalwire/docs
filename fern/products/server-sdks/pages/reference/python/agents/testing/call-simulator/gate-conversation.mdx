The dialogue a [semantic gate][semantic-gate] judges now, as `"role: text"` lines,
oldest first. The lines are the spoken turns the model sees (see
[`model_view()`][model-view]), chosen as the platform chooses them:

1. With the gate's `since` mark set, the turns from the agent's first turn at or
   after the mark.
2. Otherwise, with `dialogue` set to `"proposal"` (the gate's, or the agent's
   `semantic_gate_dialogue`) and a proposal recorded, the turns from the agent's
   first turn after `semantic_state` last changed.
3. Otherwise, the window.

Then at most `history` of the newest: the gate's, the agent's
`semantic_gate_history`, or 20. Pass it to your gate callback to judge a gate in a
test, for example `gates=lambda gate, sim, args: judge(gate["question"],
sim.gate_conversation(gate))`.

## Parameters

<ParamField path="gate" type="Mapping[str, Any] | None" default="None" toc={true}>
  The gate's dictionary, or just its `dialogue`, `history`, and `since` keys.
  `None` uses the agent's settings.
</ParamField>

## Returns

`list[str]` -- The lines, such as `"user: Yes."`.

## Raises

`NotImplementedError` once the call has entered a step with a reset, as for
`model_view()`.

## Example

```python {19}
from signalwire import AgentBase, FunctionResult
from signalwire.testing import CallSimulator

agent = AgentBase(name="orders", route="/orders")
agent.prompt_add_section("Role", "You take orders.")
agent.define_tool("review_order", "Read the order back", {"type": "object", "properties": {}},
                  lambda args, raw_data: FunctionResult("Read the order back.").swml_change_step("confirm"))
agent.define_tool("place_order", "Place the order", {"type": "object", "properties": {}},
                  lambda args, raw_data: FunctionResult("Order placed."))
ctx = agent.define_contexts().add_context("default")
ctx.add_step("take").set_text("Take the order.").set_functions(["review_order"])
ctx.add_step("confirm").set_text("Confirm the order.").set_functions(["place_order"]).set_history("isolate")

sim = CallSimulator(agent)
sim.caller("A large tea, please.")
sim.call("review_order")
sim.agent_says("That's one large tea. Shall I place it?")
sim.caller("Yes.")
print(sim.gate_conversation({"history": 2}))
# ["assistant: That's one large tea. Shall I place it?", 'user: Yes.']
```
